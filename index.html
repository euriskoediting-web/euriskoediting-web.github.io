Frequently asked questions

Is Adam Voice really free to start?

Yes. You get 300 trial credits instantly as a guest, and 1,000 free credits when you create an account. No credit card is needed for the free credits.

How do credits work?

1 credit = 1 character (including spaces). A 1,000-character script costs 1,000 credits. At $1 for 10,000 credits, a full 10-minute YouTube narration costs about one dollar.

Where does the voice generation happen?

Entirely in your browser, using the open-source Kokoro AI voice model. That is how we keep prices this low — there is no expensive server between you and the voice. Your scripts stay on your device.

Can I use the audio commercially?

Yes. Voiceovers you generate with paid or free credits can be used in your monetized videos, ads, podcasts and client work.

Do credits expire?

No. Credit packs never expire. Use them today or next year.

Which voices are included?

Every pack unlocks all voices: Adam, Michael, Onyx, Eric and Liam (US male), Daniel, George and Lewis (UK male), plus Heart, Bella and Emma (female).
